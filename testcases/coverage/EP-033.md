- 22 test cases exist for EP-033 (TC-033-001 .. TC-033-022): 12 NOT RUN, 10 BLOCKED (per `python tools/verify_claims.py --tally EP-033`).
- 3 conflicts recorded: CF-033-01 (customer-facing filter), CF-033-02 (legacy-category exclusion), CF-033-03 (tree-vs-flat-list naming) - each blocks exactly one case (TC-033-016, TC-033-008, TC-033-017 respectively).
