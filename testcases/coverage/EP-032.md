suggested test scenarios, Source agreement matrix, 2 recorded conflicts). 17 new cases added (TC-032-001..017); 2 new
flow steps added (FLOW-02, FLOW-06); 2 new Conflicts-sheet entries added (CF-032-01, CF-032-02).

missing brand/listing fixtures); 1 item (undocumented 5xx/DB-failure path) has no feasible case without fault-injection
tooling; dead response fields (slug/logo/navigationTab/createdAt) are returned and verified present but unused by any
client; the sheet's "Flow 2, 6" usage note under-represents the real 8+ mobile call sites (guest/buyer/seller; no web
call site exists at all).
