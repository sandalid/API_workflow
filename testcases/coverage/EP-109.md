## Unused-in-app (dead/never-read) items

- `AUD:unused-in-app` — **none identified.** Per audit's "Looks unused but keep" section, every response field (`isSeller`, `payout`, `taxIdLast4`, `sellerType`, `legalName`, etc.) is confirmed live-read by at least one mobile/web call site. The only field-level anomaly is the inverse case — mobile's `SellerProfile.id` is declared but never *sent* by the backend (covered above as TC-109-012), not a backend field the app ignores.

## Summary

- 15 cases total (TC-109-001 … TC-109-015): 9 NOT RUN, 6 BLOCKED.
- 6 BLOCKED reasons: no confirmed non-seller account (TC-109-003), no disposable suspended-seller fixture (TC-109-005), the CF-109-01 requirement/code conflict (TC-109-006), no session-revocation mechanism (TC-109-010), no mid-onboarding `sellerProfile:null` fixture (TC-109-011), spec has no 200 schema so schema-conformance would be vacuous (TC-109-013).
- 1 open conflict: CF-109-01 (recorded, blocks TC-109-006).
- 0 gaps left un-added: every item in the audit's Test design inputs / Source agreement matrix / Suggested test scenarios (scenarios #1-4, #6-7, #9-12) maps to a case. Scenario #5 (login-time suspension block) and #8 (admin DTO contrast) were deliberately **not** added as EP-109 cases — both exercise a *different* endpoint (`/auth/login` and `/admin/sellers*` respectively), as the audit itself flags scenario #8 as "out of strict EP-109 scope"; scenario #5 is instead reflected as a precondition step inside the Flow 16 narrative, not a standalone EP-109 case.
